## verdict: no traceable source

The claim has no traceable primary source: the only attributed origin—a 1993 'PC Professional' article by 'Lisa Birgit Holst'—was fabricated by Snopes in April 2001 as a deliberate in-joke (the name is an anagram of 'This is a big troll'), the cited 1954 book contains nothing about swallowing spiders, and the actual first appearance of the claim remains unknown, with the oldest verifiable mention so far being an April 1992 Cornell Engineer column and a March 1997 Usenet thread.
