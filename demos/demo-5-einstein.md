## verdict: misquoted

Einstein never said this; the quote originated anonymously in twelve-step recovery communities (earliest documented: Knoxville News-Sentinel, October 11, 1981, citing an Al-Anon member), appeared in slightly different form in a November 1981 Narcotics Anonymous draft ('repeating the same mistakes'), was paraphrased by Rita Mae Brown in her 1983 novel Sudden Death, and was first falsely attributed to Einstein only in 1990—35 years after his death—with no letter, lecture, interview, or paper by Einstein ever found containing it.
