## verdict: changed along the way

William James (1907) never mentioned '10 percent' or brain anatomy; he made a qualitative remark about untapped mental/physical potential, which Lowell Thomas (1936) falsely sharpened into the precise figure '10 percent of his latent mental ability' and falsely attributed the exact phrasing to James, then popular culture further mutated 'latent mental ability' into 'brain.'
